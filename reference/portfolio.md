## How to talk about these

Lead with the **business problem**, not the tooling. "I wanted to know whether the gap between referrals and treatment capacity was widening" is a strong opening. "I built a Power BI dashboard" is weak — it describes the artifact, not the thinking, and every candidate has an artifact.

Second thing they'll probe: **why you modelled it the way you did**. Have the answer ready. That question is what separates someone who followed a tutorial from someone who understands the model.
