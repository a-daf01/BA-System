# Target Roles — distilled market research

Research date: 31 July 2026. Sources: Reed, CWJobs, Totaljobs, ITJobsWatch, NHS Jobs, Civil Service Jobs.
Re-run this research at the end of month 2 — salary data shifts.

---

## The geography insight

Peterborough → London King's Cross is a **direct train, fastest ~47 minutes**, services every 15–20 minutes, 60–120+ trains a day.

This means the search radius is **Peterborough + Cambridge + London (remote/hybrid)**, not Peterborough alone. Filtering by "Peterborough" only shows a fraction of what's reachable. Always run searches three ways:
1. Peterborough + 20 miles
2. Cambridge + 20 miles
3. London with remote/hybrid filter on

## The exact gap

The £28k Graduate BI Analyst ad and the £35–40k BI Analyst ad in the same city are separated by almost nothing:

> Graduate BI Analyst asks for: Power BI, SAS *or* SQL; ability to query and maintain databases; Excel proficiency; degree-level education; analytical mind. SAS/Microstrategy/SPSS "a bonus but not essential."

> BI Analyst (£35–40k) asks for: SQL, SSAS, Power BI — and states **back-end SQL / database skills are key**.

**Translation: demonstrable SQL depth + Power BI + evidence = £7–12k.** That's the thesis of this whole 28-day plan.

## What every posting wants (both lanes)

Universal across both the requirements-BA and data-BA lanes:

1. Requirements elicitation and documentation — **BRD** is the single most-named artifact
2. As-is / to-be process analysis and gap identification
3. Translating business ↔ technical, in both directions
4. Presenting findings to management
5. UAT / acceptance criteria

Tool frequency, ranked: **Power BI → Excel → SQL → Jira → PowerPoint → BPMN → Python** (heavier data roles only).

Notably absent: ML, software engineering, algorithms. The CS degree is a qualifying checkbox for BSA roles specifically — every BSA ad asks for CS/IS/software engineering, not "CS *or* business admin."

## Public sector routes (transparent pay, real openings)

- **NHS Jobs** (jobs.nhs.uk) — Cambridgeshire & Peterborough NHS Foundation Trust runs live Power BI / SQL data analyst roles in its Informatics team. Pay is banded: **Band 5 £32,073 → £39,043**, Band 6 £39,959 → £48,117. No negotiation, no guessing.
- **Civil Service Jobs** (civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk) — major analyst employer, published bands, hybrid roles outside London. Also runs a **TechTrack Apprentice Business Analyst** programme (18 months, with BPP) and a **Level 4 Data Analyst Apprenticeship** (2 years). Too slow for a fast entry but a genuine fallback.

## ⚠️ Filter these out

A large share of "junior data analyst / trainee analyst / no experience needed" listings are **paid training courses disguised as job ads** — Newto Training, ITOL Recruit and similar. You pay for a course; the "job guarantee" is a conditional refund. They inflate the apparent size of the entry-level market.

**Rule: if there's no named employer and no stated salary, or the words "programme"/"job guarantee"/"qualifications included" appear, it's a course. Skip it.**

## Timeline reality

- Entry-level roles get *filled* fast — ~3.6 weeks average.
- But *finding* one takes longer: graduates average ~5.6 months to land a role; employers see ~280 applications per posting.
- Keep **3–6 applications live concurrently** so offers arrive in sequence rather than after one long wait.
- Apply at **70–80% match**. Job descriptions are aspirational wish-lists, not pass/fail checklists. Do not self-reject.

## Agencies to contact

UK analyst hiring runs heavily through agencies — a channel that barely exists in the Gulf market. Observed repeatedly in Cambridgeshire/London listings:

- Circle Recruitment
- Harnham (data & analytics specialists)
- Robert Half
- Bristow Holland
- Hays Specialist Recruitment

Treat a recruiter conversation as free market intelligence: they will tell you exactly what's missing from your profile for the roles you want.
