# Application Pipeline

**Rule: keep 3–6 applications live at all times.** Offers then arrive in sequence rather than after one long wait. Employers see ~280 applications per role, so volume plus quality is the only strategy that works.

**Apply at 70–80% match.** Job descriptions are aspirational wish-lists, not pass/fail checklists. Do not self-reject.

---

## Search discipline

Run every search three ways:
1. Peterborough + 20 miles
2. Cambridge + 20 miles
3. London, remote/hybrid filter on

Titles: Business Systems Analyst · Systems Analyst · Technical Business Analyst · BI Analyst · Data Analyst · Reporting Analyst · **MI Analyst** · Data Business Analyst · Insight Analyst

Boards: Reed · CWJobs · Indeed UK · LinkedIn · Totaljobs · CV-Library · jobs.nhs.uk · civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk

**Filter out course-sellers:** no named employer + no stated salary, or the words "programme" / "job guarantee" / "qualifications included" → it's a paid course, not a job.
