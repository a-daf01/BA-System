## Step 2 — Rewrite in analyst language (Day 4, 45 min) — ✅ done, see the CV

**The shape, every time:**

> **What the business problem was** → **what you did** → **what changed.**

The third part is the one that gets skipped and the only one a hiring manager reads for.
If you genuinely have no number, say the qualitative change — "the team stopped
re-keying it by hand" is a result. Do not invent a percentage.

**The translation that matters.** You currently read as a builder. Same work, analyst words:

| You'd naturally write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| Built a website for X | Gathered requirements from X, translated them into a spec, delivered against it |
| Wrote a script to do Y | Identified a manual process costing N hours, automated it, freed that time |
| Made a dashboard | Defined the business question, modelled the data to answer it, built reporting on it |
| Fixed their spreadsheet | Investigated a data quality issue, traced the root cause, corrected the source |
| Talked to the client | Elicited requirements from a non-technical stakeholder and documented them |
| Figured out what they wanted | Stakeholder engagement / requirements elicitation |
| Checked it worked | Defined acceptance criteria and ran UAT against them |

**Vocabulary the ads keep using** — reuse these words literally, not paraphrased:

`requirements gathering` · `requirements elicitation` · `stakeholder management` ·
`process mapping` · `as-is / to-be` · `gap analysis` · `data quality` · `root cause` ·
`user stories` · `acceptance criteria` · `UAT` · `data modelling` · `reporting` ·
`MI` (management information — UK-specific, appears constantly) · `insight` ·
`translating business needs into technical requirements` · `single source of truth`

> Add to this list on Day 4's phone block as you read the three new ads. Copy the exact
> phrasing you see repeated — that repetition is the keyword filter you're being scored
> against.

## Step 4 — The CV (20 min)

**Section order. Do not redesign this** — every minute spent on layout is a minute not
spent on the pipeline, and a UK recruiter reads these in about 12 seconds.

| # | Section | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name, phone, email, LinkedIn, location | "Peterborough (relocating)" or "Peterborough / remote-London" |
| 2 | **Personal statement — 3 lines max** | Who you are, what you're targeting, what you bring. Formula below |
| 3 | **Key skills** | SQL · Power BI · DAX · Power Query · data modelling · requirements gathering · UAT. A scannable line, not a grid of star ratings |
| 4 | **Projects** | Portfolio 1 and 2 land here from Days 20 and 26. On Day 6 this section says what you're building |
| 5 | Experience | Steps 1–3 bullets, most recent first |
| 6 | Education | BSc Computer Science, University of Lincoln |

**One page ideally. Two maximum. Never three.**

### Personal statement formula

> [CS graduate / analyst] with hands-on [SQL and Power BI], moving into [Business Systems
> Analysis]. Recent work includes [one concrete thing]. Looking for [role type] in
> [Peterborough, Cambridge or remote-London].

**Yours:**

>

### Things that lose you the read
- A skills grid with percentage bars. Nobody believes "SQL 80%".
- "Hardworking team player." Says nothing, costs a line.
- A photo. Not a UK convention and it invites bias.
- Two pages of a one-page career. Density is not thoroughness.

**The draft is already written.** Change the name, send it five times. Do not rewrite it
per agency — that's the procrastination this section exists to prevent.

```
Subject: Business Systems Analyst / BI Analyst — Peterborough, Cambridge or remote

Hi [Name],

I'm a Computer Science graduate moving into Business Systems Analysis and BI, and
I'm looking for roles around Peterborough and Cambridge, or remote with London
travel.

Hands-on with SQL and Power BI — I'm currently building two portfolio pieces on UK
public data, taking them from the business question through data modelling to the
report. Happy to send those over.

Two questions, if you have a minute:

1. What are you seeing most of at the moment for BSA and BI Analyst roles at the
   junior-to-mid end?
2. Looking at my profile, what's the single biggest gap you'd want closed before
   putting me forward?

Realistic on salary and available at short notice.

Thanks,
Ahmed
[phone] · [LinkedIn]
```

> **Question 2 is the one that pays.** These are people paid to place candidates, and
> most of them will answer honestly. Free market intelligence, and their answer feeds
> straight into month 2's plan.
