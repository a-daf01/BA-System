## 3. Sixty seconds, out loud

The question is *"so what have you been working on?"* — asked casually, by a recruiter, on
a phone call you were not expecting.

Cover three things and stop:

1. **Window functions** — ranking and running totals without collapsing rows.
2. **The star schema** — one fact table at a stated grain, dimensions around it.
3. **One defect you found** — the 93-versus-92 customer names, or the freight that came
   back fifty times too big.

Time it. Write the actual number here: ______ seconds.

**Over ninety seconds means cut something**, and the thing to cut is always the setup, not
the finding.
