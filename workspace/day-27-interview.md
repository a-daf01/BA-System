## 3. Five questions to ask them (20 min)

Specific to data stack and reporting maturity. **Anything about culture is a
wasted question** — everyone says the same thing.

Strong openers to adapt:
- "Where does your reporting data actually come from, and who owns it?"
- "Is there a single source of truth for [their key metric], or does each team
  have its own version?"
- "What's currently produced manually that shouldn't be?"
- "When a number in a report is questioned, who investigates it today?"
- "What does the reporting stack look like end to end — source system through to
  what the business sees?"
