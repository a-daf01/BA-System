| NHS England | "Which trusts show the widest gap between referral volume and treatment capacity, and is that gap widening?" |

| ONS | "Which regions have seen real earnings fall furthest against inflation, and how does that track against employment rate?" |
| data.gov.uk transport | "Where are the road-safety hotspots that have got worse despite intervention spending?" |

**Bad questions, for contrast — all of these fail:**
- "A dashboard about NHS waiting times." *(no metric, no decision)*
- "Analysis of council spending." *(that's a topic, not a question)*
- "Trends in the data." *(no.)*

**The test:** if you can't say what someone would *do differently* after reading
the answer, it isn't a business question yet.
